<h1>The Stacks</h1>

<p class="codex-meta">10,243 books <span class="stack-sep" aria-hidden="true">·</span> counted nightly <span class="stack-sep" aria-hidden="true">·</span> holes admitted</p>

<div markdown="1" class="dropcap">
The canon records the favourites; this page counts the whole shelf. 10,243 books, the lot, audited by a machine and annotated by the man who fed it. The canon explains what the library means; this page is what the library is, down to the holes in it. The numbers recompute nightly and the sentences stay the same, which is the honest division of labour between me and the thing I built.
</div>

## I. The Build Year
{: #the-build-year}

The whole collection entered the shelves in one year, and the machine watched it happen. 2026-10 was the loudest month at 1,964 books; even the quietest, 2026-08, added 401. A library assembled this fast is not inherited. It is built, and the curve below is the construction record.

## VII. Errata
{: #errata}

Every archive has holes; this one admits its own. 10,090 books, 99 percent, carry no rating, which is the shelf's way of saying the reading outpaces the judging. 1,013 have no ISBN on file. The catalogue answers to the census, not the reverse.

<p class="ornament ornament--asterism" aria-hidden="true">⁂</p>
